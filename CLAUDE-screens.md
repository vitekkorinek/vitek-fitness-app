## Tab bars, headers, profile layout & per-screen UI (CLAUDE.md §2)

### Client profile layout
- No avatar/profile card between header and tab bar
- **Header = `LightHeader solid` (July 2026)** — the old dark-green `SafeAreaView` header is gone; back chevron · client name · `plus`, `StatusBar dark-content`, root bg `#faf9f7`. **`solid`** = opaque (not see-through glass) because the dense week-strip ghosted through the translucent version. Session-timer indicator lives in the `LightHeader` `overlay` slot.
- Tabs: **Training / Sessions / Nutrition / Progress / Info** — default opens on Training. **Sessions is its own top-level tab** (July 2026) — the old Training/Sessions segmented toggle *inside* the Training tab was removed; `SessionsTab` is now rendered directly at `activeTab === 'sessions'`. The Training tab was too content-heavy to also host the toggle.
- **The 5-tab (main) switcher is `TabPillSwitcher`** (module-level in `index.tsx`) — a **plain UNDERLINE switcher** (July 2026 round-3): pinned just below the solid header (`top: segTop`, where `const segTop = headerH - 12` pulls it up so the title sits tighter; content pads `paddingTop: segTop + SEG_STRIP_H(50) + 12` for breathing room, `scrollIndicatorInsets`/`progressViewOffset` at `segTop + SEG_STRIP_H`). The 5 titles are evenly spread (`segItem flex:1`); the active tab gets **accent-green text (700) + a 2px accent underline under the label only** (inner `segUnderline` view hugs the text), inactive are **DIMMED — `#bbb` at weight 500** (July 26 2026, Vitek: five black labels in a row "is a lot of noise"; only the active tab should read as a live element — this also matches the client-side underline switchers, Workouts/Stretching + Body composition/Strength, which are `#bbb` inactive). **The sliding glass pill was REMOVED from here** — it read as too heavy for a 5-item primary row and clashed with the sub-tab toggle directly below. (Was: sliding `GlassView` pill → `#e7e7e3`-track pill → underline originally.)
- **Sub-tab switchers = a GLASS TOGGLE (`components/GlassToggle.tsx`), trainer side only** — a compact segmented switcher with a faint frosted track (`rgba(120,120,120,0.09)`) + a single **sliding pill that's real Liquid Glass** (`GlassView glassEffectStyle="regular"`, iOS 26) over white, frosted-white fallback off iOS 26. Fills its parent width, options split evenly, `Animated.spring` translateX. Used by **Nutrition** (Planning/Overview — `nutrition-tab.tsx`, `s.subToggle`) and **Progress** (Body composition/Strength — new `variant="glass"` branch in `ProgressTab`). Net two-level hierarchy: **underline = primary tabs, glass toggle = secondary sub-tabs** (never confused). **The client-side Progress screen is UNCHANGED — it still passes `variant="client"` (underline).**

### Client Progress tab — a HUB, not a tab switcher (`app/(client)/(tabs)/progress.tsx`, Aug 6 2026)

The client Progress screen is the app's **second animated landing**, after nutrition's Library. It is no longer a thin wrapper that renders `ProgressTab` with a switcher: it owns a `view` state (`'landing' | 'composition' | 'strength' | 'measurements' | 'comparison' | 'consistency'`) and five folders arranged in the Library's pentagon.

**The centre object is a body the readings BLOOM off.** At the crown, the hands and the feet a **reading detaches** (leaving a pulse ring) and flies to its slot, crown→hands→feet over 700ms. The figure never leaves — same as the book staying open. Vitek proposed either a spreading/unrolling figure or balls out of a graph; both were talked down and he agreed after a mockup. The rule the book taught — **what flies out must be made of the same stuff as the centre** — holds here because *a reading taken off a body* is literally what a stat card is. A spreading figure with badges on its fingertips reads as HOLDING them, and a head that flies away leaves a headless torso.

- **⚠️ THE LANDING DOES NOT SCAN, AND MUST NOT (Aug 7 2026).** It did until then — a line swept the figure head→feet and lit the strip it crossed — and the animation was well liked. It came out anyway, on Vitek's own argument when he specced the Strength scan: **a scan should mean a reading is being TAKEN, and the landing measures nothing.** Spend the mechanic here and it stops meaning anything in the Strength folder, where the body genuinely is being read. What survives is the part that always carried the idea: five pulse rings leaving the body in order, each with its badge. The lit-band copy of the figure and the glowing line are **deleted**, not disabled.
  - **The cadence was KEPT.** `BLOOM_PAD` is the old scan band's height, still shaping `bloomAt()` so the crown does not fire on frame 0 nor the feet on the last frame. That rhythm was tuned on device and is the part Vitek liked — do not "simplify" it to a bare `fy`, which snaps both ends flat. **⚠️ It is held as a FRACTION of `FIG_H` (0.15, = the original 30 over the original 200)**, so resizing the body leaves the cadence exactly where it was tuned; a hardcoded 30 silently shortens the lead-in every time the figure grows.
  - The lesson the band taught lives on in the Strength scan: **a sweep must clear the silhouette at both ends.** v1 of the landing stopped it level with the feet and they stayed lit for as long as the figure was on screen — Vitek: *"the scan finished at his feet so they stay light green, like shoes haha"*.
- **⚠️ AND IT MUST NOT SPIN.** Proposed in the same breath as dropping the scan ("it would just spin around its axes and the badges would fly out of it"). Two reasons it is worse: `BodyMap` is two FLAT SVGs, so a "spin" can only be a front→back flip — and **that flip is the mechanic the Strength scan needs** for the posterior chain, so spending it here costs exactly what the scan did; and this hub is deliberately 2D, so a 3D transform buys back every rotateY / perspective / coplanar-flicker trap that cost nine device rounds on the book.
- **⚠️ `hubW` is `width - 32`, so the landing's contentContainer MUST carry `paddingHorizontal: 16`.** Without it the container is full-bleed while every position is computed against the inset width, and the whole hub sits exactly 16pt left of centre. That is how it shipped in the first OTA.
- **⚠️ An interpolation `inputRange` must be STRICTLY increasing.** The feet anchor is late enough in the sweep that an unclamped `start + 0.22` lands on 1 and duplicates the final stop — that throws at render, it does not merely look wrong. The pulse windows are clamped inside (0,1).
- Landing content is `flexGrow: 1` + `justifyContent: 'center'` so the hub centres between header and tab bar rather than hanging off the top. **⚠️ Plus an OPTICAL lift — the block carries `marginBottom: 68` and it is not a bug to fix.** The badges are airy circles and the body is one solid dark mass, so the hub's visual weight sits below its measured middle and a true geometric centre reads as sagging (*"maybe is centered but it needs to be higher visually a bit"*, Aug 8 2026). **It is doubled on purpose: centring splits the margin, so the block rises by half of whatever is put there.**
- **Badges are ROUND, not page-shaped** (`CHIP_D` 70, smaller than the Library's 78×94 page, which is what buys room for the bigger figure). Vitek: *"maybe we dont have to have cards/pages like with library, it can be rounded badges - that could go good with the rounded impulses coming out of the body"*. Each wears a faint accent ring — the pulse that fired off the body, stopped. **Do NOT put tick marks or a rounded-square back:** that was the page-rule analogue and it reads as a card, which is the Library's language.
- **Pure 2D — translate/scale/opacity only.** No layer is ever 3D-transformed, so none of the rotateY / perspective / coplanar-flicker traps from the book apply.
- The figure is **`components/BodyMap.tsx`** at **170×340** (`scale 0.85`, grown from 0.50 over two rounds on Aug 8 2026 — *"doesnt look that much bigger, we can try more"*), `regions={[]}`. `BodyMap`'s optional **`baseFill`** prop (flat-fills the whole body in one colour) was added for the deleted scan band and now has **no caller** — kept because it is the natural way to build any future full-body reveal. Leave it unset for a normal body.
  - **⚠️ WHAT CAPS THE FIGURE IS THE SIDE BADGES' TEXT — not the badge circles, not the screen.** "Measurements" / "Arms · waist · thighs" are ~105 wide, so at `sideX` 122.5 their inner edge is only ~70 off centre, while the body's widest point by far is the **splayed fingers** (`0.45 × FIG_W`, against `0.26` at the chest). Those labels sat at exactly hand height, and that — nothing else — is what pinned the body at 100 wide. **The fix was to move the side pair UP to flank the shoulders, not to shave the body**; their text now lands beside the chest and the arms own that latitude. Try the same before ever shrinking the figure again. **⚠️ The side pair is now AT ITS FLOOR** — its sub-line ends just **6** above the fingertips, and its inner edge already overlaps them horizontally, so that gap is the only thing keeping them apart (Vitek spent the clearance asking for *"one tic lower"*). Anything lower needs the labels moved off the hands' latitude, not a smaller number. **The bottom pair is the knob with room** in both directions — the block just grows.
  - **⚠️ The ring is DERIVED from body landmarks, never hand-placed** (`ring` useMemo). `CROWN_FY -0.433` / `FEET_FY 0.426` are the artwork's own top and bottom inside its box (the viewBox carries 6.7% of padding), and `HAND_TOP_FY -0.045` / `HAND_BOT_FY 0.080` bracket the splayed hands — **the band no side badge may enter, and the single constraint the whole ring is built around**. Every slot reads "clear of *this* part by *this much* air": composition hangs `14 + CHIP_TEXT_H` above the crown, the side pair the same above the fingertips, the bottom pair `30 + 35` below them; `HUB_BLOCK_H`/`hubCy` fall out of the same arithmetic. **Do not replace these with the numbers they evaluate to today** — that is what made the first resizes a manual re-tune of five badges. `CHIP_TEXT_H` (79) is the label+sub hanging below a badge's centre, so a font change is the one thing that can invalidate the ring.
  - **⚠️ The SIDE pair cannot go lower — asked and answered (Aug 8 2026).** Vitek proposed dropping Strength/Measurements so the bottom pair could rise: *"should we put strenght and measurements down and comparison consistency higher since they are a bit shorter words?"* Their sub-line already ends **14pt above the fingertips**; it is the same hand-width fact that sent them up in the first place. **The bottom pair was the one with slack** — parked below the FEET when nothing required it, leaving ~160 of dead side space. Raised 68 so its circle sits beside the thighs and its text beside the ankles; it only has to clear the *bottom* of the hand band, its words are short, and the shins are the narrowest the body gets.
- **`progress` is in `NO_SHARED_HEADER`** (`(tabs)/_layout.tsx`) — a hub's title changes with the open folder and its back chevron returns to the landing, neither of which the shared header can know. It renders its own `LightHeader`, which falls back to the default session/rest chips.
- Body composition and Strength mount the existing content via **`ProgressTab`'s new `embeddedTab` prop** (one sub-tab, no switcher). The extraction of `MeasurementsSubTab`/`StrengthSubTab` out of the 2500-line trainer file was **deliberately deferred** — `embeddedTab` got the hub on device without pulling it apart.
- **⚠️ NAMING TRAP: `MeasurementsSubTab` in `progress-tab.tsx` IS Body composition** (scale/BIA numbers) and only carries that name for historical reasons. The tape tab is `components/TapeMeasurementsTab.tsx`. Rename the old one `BodyCompSubTab` when the trainer side is ported.
#### Strength folder — the muscle scan (`components/MuscleScanCard.tsx`, Aug 7 2026)

**This is what the scan is FOR.** **Mounted INSIDE `ProgressTab`'s strength branch — both sides, from one place** (ported to the trainer Aug 7 2026). It started outside, in the client screen, while it was client-only; that mount was **removed** in the same change, or the client would see the card twice. It scans once on mount, and the sub-tab is only mounted the first time it is opened, so the animation plays when it is first seen rather than while hidden. Front and back `BodyMap` figures side by side at `scale 0.62`; one line crosses **both** (two lines would read as two scans) and each muscle lights at the moment the line reaches it.

- **The body carries the HEAT, the list carries the NUMBERS.** Vitek's spec had leader lines shooting from each muscle to a label at the side. Measured against live data first: one week lit **22 distinct muscles / 9 body parts**, and 22 leader lines around a 250pt figure is a medical diagram. Tapping a list row instead lights that one part **alone at full strength** — the same connection, on demand, costing no space. Leader lines for the top two or three remain open if the list reads as disconnected on device.
- **Nine body parts** (`BODY_PARTS` in `lib/muscleVolume.ts`): eight are `MUSCLE_FILTER_OPTIONS`, reached through the exported **`muscleFilterLabels()`** so there is no second copy of `MUSCLE_MAP` (CLAUDE.md §8). Two forced deviations: **Forearms is ours** (not a filter chip, but 4 exercises carry it as primary and 22 as secondary, so the filter map alone drops that work silently), and **'Full Body' is dropped** (a workout category, not a muscle; no exercise stores it; there is no honest way to light "everything").
- **Heat is 5 steps, RELATIVE to the busiest part in the window**, all in brand green (`HEAT_RAMP`). Amber and red already mean bonus and danger app-wide, so a hot-cold ramp through them would say something it doesn't mean. `BodyMap`'s `colors` prop is now `string[]` instead of a 2-tuple — its fill lookup always clamped to the palette length, so a 5-step ramp needed only the type widening.
- **Primary sets rank and heat; secondary only assists.** A part with assist work and no primary reads `"N assist"` and is floored at heat 1 — real work must not read as untrained, but it has not earned a rank.
- **The reveal is state, not a transform.** The line is native-driven; a listener on the same `Animated.Value` sets `revealY` **only when a muscle actually crosses** (~8 renders per sweep, not one per frame). Each region's trigger is its `MUSCLE_YFOCUS`. "Lit" is a change of *which* regions `BodyMap` renders, so it cannot be a style animation.
- **Week = Mon–Sun, Month = calendar month**, matching every other weekly readout in the app. A rolling 7 days reads better on a Monday morning and was rejected for exactly that inconsistency.
- An **untrained** line closes the list, and every name in it is tappable. An unlit muscle is indistinguishable from an untracked one, so the gap has to be said in words — it is also the most useful thing on the card for a client.
- **⚠️ A tap on the figure answers about the MUSCLE, a tap on a row about the body PART, and the difference is load-bearing** (Aug 7 2026). Calves lives inside Legs, which was the hottest part of Vitek's first real week — answering at part level would have told him "Legs · 39 sets" for a muscle that got nothing. He asked for exactly this: *"if i click on core and it wasnt worked or calfves btw! it should say not trained or something over the person"*. So `fetchMuscleScan` also returns per-slug volume (`muscles`), computed **independently of the region accumulation** — that one drops a secondary muscle whose part already has primary work, which is right for the heat map and would wrongly answer "not trained" for the adductors in a set of lunges.
- **The answer appears ABOVE the figures, never below the list.** A fixed-height strip (so nothing jumps) showing e.g. `Quads · 30 sets · 2×`, or `Calves — not trained this week` in **red**, with the muscle itself painted red on the body. Vitek on the old grey footer line: *"the light grey writing is not noticible"*. ⚠️ **Red means "missing" ONLY here and only while something is selected** — everywhere else in the app red is danger — so the resting card never shows any. It rides one step past the heat ramp (`MISSING_LEVEL`), which is why the palette prop had to accept any length.
- **⚠️ A SELECTED MUSCLE KEEPS ITS OWN HEAT — it is not forced to full accent** (Aug 8 2026). It used to be pinned to `HEAT_RAMP.length`, so a barely-trained muscle jumped to the strongest green the moment you tapped it: the tap looked like it was answering *how much*, and answering it wrongly. Every colour on this body is functional (five heat steps plus red for never-trained), and **the selection reads because everything ELSE goes dark, not because the subject changes** — the same rule Vitek set for the limb view's amber/red, extended to the whole ramp: *"keep the color that shows when selecting certain body part … those are all functional colors here"*.
- **The "% more weight than this time last week/month" line** (`fetchVolumeTrend` in `lib/muscleVolume.ts`, Aug 8 2026). The card said WHAT was trained and never whether it was going anywhere, so Vitek asked for Consistency's comparison here too: *"how much more percent they lift, if there is progress, per week and per month in comparison to the previous week or month"*. Same sentence shapes and same ±3% dead band as Consistency's `insight`, so the two screens speak with one voice.
  - **⚠️ THE PREVIOUS PERIOD IS CLIPPED TO THE SAME NUMBER OF DAYS ELAPSED** (`previousPeriodRange`) — a partial current week measured against a complete previous one reports a collapse every Monday and a recovery every Sunday, which is the calendar talking, not the training. Tuesday is compared with last Tuesday-so-far, and the phrase **"this time last week"** is what makes that visible rather than hidden — do not shorten it to "last week". Months clip by day-of-month with a `min` guard: on the 31st the previous month may not have one, and `new Date(y, m, 31)` silently rolls forward.
  - Its own RPC round trip (`client_volume_by_date`), deliberately not folded into the scan's — different question, different source — and it fails to `null` so the card still works without it. **⚠️ Never sum volume from `session_logs` client-side**: one live client has 1283 rows against PostgREST's 1000-row cap, so the total comes back plausibly wrong rather than empty.
- **⚠️ THE NUMBERS ARRIVE AFTER THE SWEEP, NOT WITH IT** (Aug 8 2026). The list used to render the instant the data landed, so it was already sitting there while the line was still crossing the chest — the card appeared to report findings it had not taken yet: *"the scan happen slightly after the values appear under it, can we have first scan and then the values? makes more sense"*. `listAnim` fades it in from the sweep's completion callback. **⚠️ The list is MOUNTED throughout at opacity 0** so the card is full height from the start — do not "simplify" this to conditional rendering, which puts a layout jump exactly where the eye is.
- **⚠️ A second tap on the same muscle does NOTHING; tapping the empty space around the figures is what clears** (Aug 8 2026). Tapping a muscle asks a question about it, and asking twice should not undo the answer — the old toggle meant a mis-registered second tap silently threw you back to the whole-body view: *"typing on it again doesnt do anything just stay as is, and typing outside of the silhouette lights up the baseline again"*. The `s.figures` container is the clear target (a `Pressable`); the muscle zones are its children and RN gives the deepest view the responder first, so a tap ON a muscle never reaches it. **The list rows still toggle** — that is standard list behaviour and he only asked about the body.
- **⚠️ The scan plays once per window and nothing replays it by hand.** Tapping the figure used to — *"tapping on the silhouette it makes scan again which is weird, once is enough in this scenario"*. Switching Week/Month reloads and legitimately scans again.
- **`PART_SLUGS` is a deliberate second copy of a mapping** (body part → figure muscles), the one place the "never duplicate a map" rule is broken on purpose: it is reached only for a part with NO work, where there are no regions to derive it from precisely because nothing was trained.

#### Body composition folder — the reading ring (`components/BodyCompRing.tsx`, Aug 7 2026, CLIENT ONLY)

**The scan is earned here** — these numbers come off a Tanita, so the body genuinely was scanned. Same test before putting the animation anywhere else. Six readings ring the figure (Weight · Fat · Muscle · Water · Visceral · BMR), three a side, each leaving the body as the line reaches its row.

- **⚠️ THE READINGS RING THE BODY, THEY ARE NOT PLACED ON IT.** Weight, water, visceral fat and BMR are located nowhere — a badge beside the left arm reading "BMR 1650" is a lie about geography. Only fat and muscle have a limb breakdown, and that stays further down, on the figure, where it is true. Do not "improve" this by anchoring the six to body parts.
- **⚠️ The "Measured … · Added by …" caption sits BELOW the graph card on the client, ABOVE it on the trainer** (Aug 8 2026). It is a footnote, not a heading — you ask when a reading was taken *after* you have read the number — and parked between the body and its graph it pushed the graph down the screen for nothing: *"can we have the measure date infor under the graph card so the graph can be a bit higher still?"*. `metricTabHint` keeps its own bottom margin for the trainer's stacking, so the client instance overrides the spacing at the call site rather than changing the shared style.
- **Tapping a badge selects; the existing graph for that metric appears underneath.** No new screen, no popup. The popup was considered and rejected: those charts have drag-to-scrub tooltips, and scrubbing inside a glass popup inside a ScrollView is the combination that already forced `onScrollLock` onto this screen. It reuses `activeMetric` untouched, so every chart, zone bar and goal keeps working.
- **⚠️ ONE FILE, DIFFERENT TOP** (Vitek's decision). The trainer keeps the dense 6-tile grid, the client gets the ring, and **only the picker differs** — graphs, history, add form and quick edit are shared. He arrives from the Tanita with fifteen numbers to type and wants a table; the client wants to understand one number and reads a body better. Chosen because it is REVERSIBLE: if he prefers the ring for entry too, the conditional is what goes.
- **Layout (round 2, Aug 7):** a wide **weight + height pill above the figure**, then BMR · FAT flanking the head, MUSCLE · WATER flanking the legs, VISCERAL centred below. **Weight is NOT a badge** — it pairs with height in the pill because those two are the numbers the CLIENT owns, and they are what you know before any scan. **⚠️ The pill has two targets: tapping it SELECTS weight like any badge, the pencil EDITS.** The first build had a full-width "Log today's weight" button that did the editing and ate a whole row — *"takes a lot of space"*.
- **⚠️ The figure is SIZED OFF THE SCREEN, never hardcoded** (Aug 8 2026, when it grew from `scale 0.5` on Vitek's ask). It is boxed in horizontally by the two badge columns, so `FIG_W` is `width − 32 (page padding) − 2×COL_W − 2×GUTTER`, clamped to 100…168; `FIG_SCALE` and `FIG_H` derive from it (the artwork is always 1:2). Keep the arithmetic tied to those constants — a fixed width that fits Vitek's phone overflows the row on a smaller one, and **it is the badges that get pushed off, not the body**.
  - **The room came out of `COL_W`, which was over-wide at 96.** The longest label is "VISCERAL" (~55), so the column only ever needed that plus a zone word; the surplus was whitespace between badge and body. It is **88** now, with an 8pt gutter.
  - **⚠️ `col` needs an explicit `height` or its `space-between` does nothing.** That rule was declared from the start and sat inert while the column happened to match the figure's height. Once the body grew, a content-sized column left the badges clustered round the torso with the head and shins unflanked — which is not a ring. `FIG_H × 0.84` keeps the two circles at ~0.17 and ~0.7 down the body (chest and thigh), where they read before the resize.
- **⚠️ BMI IS GONE FROM BOTH SIDES** — Vitek's judgement, *"i dont believe in that (its outdated)"*, extended to the trainer on his say-so the same day. Weight has no zones now and is always a plain trend line; the WEIGHT tile carries no zone badge. `getBmiSegs` is left in the file unused — **do not re-wire it without asking him**. **Weight KEPT its graph** although he wondered whether it needed one (*"i am not sure if kg needs a graph?"*) — it is the number that moves most often and the one clients weigh themselves for.
- **⚠️ GOALS ARE GONE FROM BOTH SIDES** — *"for now i would not use goals because that is hard to do with metrics"*, also extended to the trainer the same day. The Set-goal link, the goal modal, the `client_goals` read and the whole `goals` map are deleted. **The `client_goals` TABLE still exists and still holds rows** — nothing reads it. The goal-marker wiring inside the zone bar and the graph is kept and simply fed `null`, so bringing goals back is a UI job, not a rebuild.
- **A client may log their own WEIGHT and HEIGHT** (*"weight is something that the person could edit himself coz they can weight themselves"*). Height lives on their own profile row, which they are allowed to update; **⚠️ the freshly saved value must win LOCALLY** (`heightOverride`), because the profile reaching this screen is a prop the parent never refetches. **⚠️ It always writes a row the CLIENT authored — never an edit of the trainer's.** A client may only change a measurement whose `created_by` is their own id, so updating the latest row would match zero rows and fail silently, which is the bug this whole area came from. It also keeps the sources honestly separate: the history list already labels each row by who entered it, so a bathroom-scale weight never masquerades as a Tanita reading.
#### The limb view — fat / muscle per segment (Aug 7 2026, both sides)

The hand-rolled `#9eaab8` rectangle-man is gone; `BodySilhouette` draws `BodyMap` at scale 0.5, lights each measured segment, and sweeps a scan line once (replaying when you switch fat ↔ muscle — a different reading off the same body).

- **⚠️ THE TINT MEANS IMBALANCE, NOT SIZE.** Colouring a limb by how many kg it holds would paint the trunk hottest on every human alive — a torso outweighing an arm is anatomy, not information. A measured segment lights flat mint; only the **heavier half of an uneven pair** takes amber (>5%) or red (>10%), the same thresholds and colours as the dots on the cards. The trunk is never tinted: nothing to be uneven against.
- **⚠️ `BodyRegion.bodySide` names the PERSON's side, and BodyMap owns the mirror.** On the front view the person's right arm is drawn on the viewer's LEFT; on the back view they coincide. Getting it backwards silently swaps every left/right reading **and the picture still looks right** — verified against the real path data (front biceps splits 2 paths, quads 3+3, forearm 3+3, calves 2+2, all mirroring correctly). Callers always say which side of the BODY they mean.
- **⚠️ EVERY tappable silhouette in the app uses OVERLAY RECTANGLES, never SVG-path `onPress`** (Aug 7 2026, after two rounds of "still hard to tap"). A path only registers a hit **inside its exact outline**, so a finger lands in the gaps between shapes; a calf is a few points across at any size the screen allows. **Making the drawing bigger does not fix a hit area shaped like a calf** — that was tried first and failed. Boxes are percentages of the figure, deliberately coarser than the anatomy, later entries winning on overlap so the more specific muscle is listed last. **Do not reshape them to follow the silhouette.** Applies to the limb view (5 zones) and the Strength scan (`FRONT_ZONES` / `BACK_ZONES`). `BodyMap`'s `onPressMuscle` still exists and still works — it is simply not how these two screens do it.
- **⚠️ Tapping a limb SELECTS the body part, for BOTH roles.** The zones were originally created only when the trainer's edit handler was passed, which left the client's body completely inert — *"the body composition silhouette per body part doesnt work at all the tap"*. Editing still belongs to the CARD (trainer only); the body answers "which one is this", a question either of them can ask. **⚠️ SELECTING NEVER CHANGES THE SUBJECT'S COLOUR — the OTHER parts drop away, and that is the whole signal** (Aug 8 2026). The resting body used to be a light mint (`#b8ded1`) with a 4th "selected" accent step, so a tap flipped the part light→dark green *and* dimmed everything else — two changes at once, and Vitek read the new green as a new meaning: *"its confusing that before its light green and then suddenly its dark green when only part is selected"*. **Measured is now the full ACCENT at all times**, so the ramp is `[ACCENT, amber, red]` and `posLevel` no longer knows about selection. This generalises the rule that already governed uneven pairs — **⚠️ an IMBALANCED part keeps its amber/red when selected, and the card's outline and fill match the dot** (*"if there is a imbalance tapping on that part can stay yellow? … or red of course"*). **Selecting is a way of asking about something; it must not overwrite what the thing is telling you.** Corollary worth keeping: **the only colours on this body mean something** — do not reintroduce a decorative second green, it will be read as if it does. Wiring `onPress` onto each muscle path looked correct and failed on device (*"doesnt work the tapping"*; on the Strength figure *"not responsive sometimes … especially calves"*). **An SVG path only registers a hit inside its exact outline**, and a calf at this size is a few points across, so the finger lands in the gaps between shapes. #### IN PROGRESS session card — BOTH sides (July 28 2026)
**A session that has been STARTED and not finished must render on its day.** Both week strips fetched `.in('status', ['completed','scheduled'])` only, and starting a planned workout **converts that very row** to `in_progress` — so a running session was neither planned nor done and **disappeared from its day entirely**, with no way back into it. - **⚠️ THE RULE IS ENFORCED IN DO MODE, NOT JUST ON THE CARD (July 28 2026, second pass).** The card check alone was walked around immediately: Vitek signed out of the trainer side, signed in as the client, and **the header `SessionResumeChip` was still counting his trainer session** — tapping it went straight into Do Mode and showed the real weights, while the card next to it refused. ### Banner photo editor (Account + Info tab)

Both `account.tsx` and `app/(trainer)/client/[id]/index.tsx` (InfoTab) use identical banner editor logic:

The `users` SELECT policy is `(id = auth.uid()) OR is_trainer()`, so **a client cannot read the trainer's `users` row** — any `from('users').eq('role','trainer')` query returns null for a logged-in client. - **Rendering:** `baseH = round(naturalH × containerW / naturalW)`. Both dimensions scale uniformly: `imageW = containerW × zoom`, `imageH = max(PREVIEW_HEIGHT, round(baseH × zoom))`. Centered horizontally: `left = -round(containerW × (zoom−1) / 2)`. Uses `resizeMode="stretch"` — no internal cropping.
- **Drag:** RNGH `GestureDetector` + `Gesture.Pan().runOnJS(true).minDistance(0)`. `overflow = max(baseH × zoom − PREVIEW_HEIGHT, 0)`. drag reads `bannerBaseHRef` and `bannerZoomRef` (mutable refs, updated each render).
- **Zoom buttons (−/+):** range 1.0–2.5. Center-preserving: `newOffsetY = clamp(round(offsetY × oldOverflow / newOverflow), 0, 100)` where overflows use `baseH × zoom`.
- **Saved fields:** `banner_photo_url`, `banner_photo_offset_y` (0–100, default 50), `banner_photo_zoom` (1.0–2.5, default 1.0) on the `users` record.

## 16. Auth Screens (`app/(auth)/`)

### Password field eye toggle
- All password inputs have a show/hide eye toggle: `SymbolView name={show ? 'eye.slash' : 'eye'} size={20}` (expo-symbols), absolutely positioned `right:14` inside the field, field gets `paddingRight:48`. Files: `login.tsx`, `signup.tsx`, `reset-password.tsx`, and the field modal inside `change-password.tsx` (`showDraft` state, reset to false on `openField`). One `showPassword` toggle can control multiple fields on the same screen (signup, reset).

### Forced first-login password change (`app/change-password.tsx`)
- The gate clients hit when `must_change_password === true` (first login / after a trainer reset). Two tappable rows (New password · Confirm password) → white centered per-field modal (eye toggle) → green "Save password" pill.
- **Header = glass green-tint `LightHeader`** (July 2026 — was a solid dark-green bar). `<LightHeader title="Set your password" />` rendered LAST over the content (no back button — it's a forced gate); root is a plain `View` bg `#faf9f7`, `StatusBar dark-content`, body offset by `useHeaderHeight() + 14` so the card starts below the green wash.
- **`handleSave` MUST route explicitly — do NOT rely on `onAuthStateChange` (July 2026 stuck-spinner fix).** Order: (1) `supabase.auth.updateUser({ password })` (surface any error — e.g. `same_password`), (2) `users.update({ must_change_password: false })`, (3) `await refreshProfile()` then `router.replace('/(client)')`. The old code left `saving=true` and depended on the `onAuthStateChange` callback re-fetching the profile to route away — but that callback runs a Supabase query while `updateUser` still holds the auth lock, which can hang, so the Save spinner spun forever. `refreshProfile()` (from `useAuth`) runs the fetch OUTSIDE the callback (lock already released) and updates the context profile so `_layout.tsx` won't bounce back to this screen; the explicit `router.replace` navigates immediately. **Never revert to the "leave saving=true, let `_layout` route via onAuthStateChange" pattern.**
- Native-ish auth-gate screen → needs a **new build** to reach TestFlight (no hot-patch).

### Forgot / Reset password flow
- **`login.tsx`** "Forgot password?" → `router.push('/(auth)/forgot-password')`. Both `forgot-password` and `reset-password` are registered in `app/(auth)/_layout.tsx`.
- **`forgot-password.tsx`:** email → `supabase.auth.resetPasswordForEmail(email, { redirectTo: Linking.createURL('/reset-password') })` (`redirectTo` = `vitekfitnessapp://reset-password`). Shows a "check your email" confirmation on success. Never reveal whether the account exists.
- **`reset-password.tsx`:** new + confirm password (eye toggle, min 8, must match) → `supabase.auth.updateUser({ password })` → `clearPasswordRecovery()`. Guards with `expired` state when there is no recovery session (`!session`).
- **Recovery deep-link handling lives in `context/AuthContext.tsx`:** the client has `detectSessionInUrl: false`, so a `Linking` listener + `getInitialURL()` parse the recovery tokens from the URL **fragment** (`#access_token=…&refresh_token=…&type=recovery`, via `parseAuthParams`), call `setSession`, and set `passwordRecovery = true`. Context exposes `passwordRecovery` + `clearPasswordRecovery()`.
- **Routing (`app/_layout.tsx`):** `if (passwordRecovery)` forces `/(auth)/reset-password` **before** all session/role routing; `passwordRecovery` is in the effect deps. `signOut()` also clears the flag.
- **External config requirement (not in code):** `vitekfitnessapp://reset-password` (+ `vitekfitnessapp://*`) MUST be in Supabase **Authentication → URL Configuration → Redirect URLs**, or GoTrue ignores `redirectTo` and falls back to the Site URL. App scheme is `vitekfitnessapp` (app.json).
- These changes require a **new build** to reach TestFlight — deep links + auth screens don't hot-patch an installed build.
